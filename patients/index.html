Become a patient representative

Your experience can help make our research more relevant for patients and their families.

Why we involve patients

Our research is about how disease and treatment affect everyday life. Only patients and their relatives know what that is really like, so we want people with that experience to help shape our studies from the start.

Patient representatives help us ask the right questions, choose questionnaires that cover what matters, and explain our results in a way that makes sense to the people they concern.

Interested?

Send us a short email with your name and phone number, and tell us whether you are a patient or a relative. We will contact you for an informal talk.

OUH.QoLReC@rsyd.dk

Please do not include details about your health or treatment in your email.

What a patient representative does

  • Comment on questionnaires

    Read questionnaires and tell us whether the questions are relevant, clear and not too burdensome.

  • Review study materials

    Give feedback on patient information, consent forms and plans for new studies.

  • Join meetings

    Take part in occasional meetings, in person or online, with researchers and other representatives.

  • Help share results

    Help us write plain-language summaries so our findings reach patients and the public.

Who can take part

  • People who have, or have had, a hematologic disease such as multiple myeloma, lymphoma, leukemia, ITP or AIHA.
  • Relatives and caregivers of people with a hematologic disease.
  • No research experience is needed. We explain everything along the way.

Practical information

  • You decide how much time you want to spend, and you can stop at any time.
  • The expected time commitment is about 8 to 10 hours every six months.
  • Travel expenses are reimbursed. The role is voluntary and unpaid.

QoLRC is part of the Department of Hematology at Odense University Hospital and is affiliated with the University of Southern Denmark.

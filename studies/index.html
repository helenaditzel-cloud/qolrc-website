Ongoing studies

Selected ongoing and early-phase studies led by or involving QoLRC.

Nordic Myeloma Study Group (NMSG) studies

  • Multiple myelomaClinicalTrials.gov: NCT04939844

    REST: isatuximab with minimal dexamethasone

    A phase 2 study of isatuximab combined with bortezomib and lenalidomide with minimal dexamethasone in patients with multiple myeloma who are not eligible for stem cell transplantation.

    Our role: Quality of life data, design, management and analysis (NMSG core facility)

  • Multiple myelomaClinicalTrials.gov: NCT04717700

    SABLe: selinexor in newly diagnosed myeloma

    A phase 2 study of selinexor with alternating bortezomib or lenalidomide plus dexamethasone in newly diagnosed patients who are not eligible for stem cell transplantation.

    Our role: Quality of life data, design, management and analysis (NMSG core facility)

  • Multiple myelomaClinicalTrials.gov: NCT04513639

    REMNANT: quality of life in MRD-guided myeloma treatment

    A Nordic randomized trial in newly diagnosed multiple myeloma, led by Oslo University Hospital, in which treatment is guided by measurable residual disease. QoLRC leads the Danish health-related quality of life work, including the statistical analysis plans for the HRQoL sub-study.

    Our role: Quality of life data, design, management and analysis (NMSG core facility)

  • Multiple myeloma

    SHIELD: subcutaneous immunoglobulin against early infections in multiple myeloma

    A randomized phase 2 trial (NMSG 32/23) testing whether preventive subcutaneous immunoglobulin reduces infections and early mortality compared with standard infection prophylaxis in patients with newly diagnosed multiple myeloma who are not eligible for stem cell transplantation. Quality of life is measured repeatedly during the first year of treatment.

    Our role: Quality of life data, design, management and analysis (NMSG core facility)

  • Multiple myelomaClinicalTrials.gov: NCT03376672

    Patient-reported outcomes in the Nordic IRd trial

    Analysis of quality of life, myeloma-specific symptoms and peripheral neuropathy in a Nordic Myeloma Study Group phase 2 trial in newly diagnosed, transplant-eligible patients, using the EORTC QLQ-C30, QLQ-MY20 and QLQ-CIPN20.

    Our role: Quality of life data, design, management and analysis (NMSG core facility)

  • Multiple myelomaClinicalTrials.gov: NCT06505369

    TALTEC: talquetamab and teclistamab consolidation

    A phase 2 study of consolidation with the bispecific antibodies talquetamab and teclistamab as part of first-line treatment in transplant-eligible patients, measuring measurable residual disease (MRD) negativity.

    Our role: Quality of life data, design, management and analysis (NMSG core facility)

Other ongoing studies

  • Multiple myelomaClinicalTrials.gov: NCT02892383

    QoL-MM: quality of life in Danish patients with multiple myeloma

    A nationwide observational study of quality of life in 681 Danish patients with newly diagnosed or relapsed multiple myeloma, followed with patient-reported outcomes for two years outside clinical trials.

    Our role: Sponsor and study lead

  • Hematologic cancerClinicalTrials.gov: NCT06373471

    IMPROVE: geriatric assessment in older patients with frailty and hematologic cancer

    A randomized controlled trial of whether adding comprehensive geriatric assessment and treatment to standard care improves quality of life and physical functioning in patients aged 70 or older with frailty and hematologic cancer. The trial aims to include 152 patients. It builds on the completed IMPROVE pilot study (NCT06689332).

    Our role: Sponsor and study lead (PI Henrik Frederiksen, PhD student Nina Andersen)

  • Multiple myeloma

    Revision of the EORTC QLQ-MY20

    Revision of the EORTC questionnaire module for patients with multiple myeloma, so that it reflects the symptoms and side effects of modern myeloma treatment. To do: Check this description.

Studies in an early phase

  • Early phaseMultiple myeloma

    ISOLDE

    An investigator-initiated, single-arm, open-label, multicenter phase 2 study of isatuximab, iberdomide and limited bortezomib in patients with newly diagnosed multiple myeloma who are not eligible for high-dose melphalan with autologous stem cell transplantation.

    Our role: Quality of life data, design, management and analysis (NMSG core facility)

  • Early phaseAutoimmune cytopenias

    Validation of the Steroid Symptom Questionnaire in ITP and AIHA

    A multinational psychometric validation study of a questionnaire measuring symptoms related to corticosteroid treatment in patients with immune thrombocytopenia and autoimmune hemolytic anemia, with planned sites in Denmark, Norway, Sweden and the United Kingdom.

    Our role: Principal investigator

QoLRC

About the center

The Quality of Life Research Center (QoLRC) studies how hematologic diseases and their treatment affect patients' everyday lives. We work with patient-reported outcomes (PROs) and health-related quality of life (HRQoL) in multiple myeloma, other hematologic cancers and benign hematologic conditions such as autoimmune cytopenias.

The center is based in the Department of Hematology at Odense University Hospital and is affiliated with the University of Southern Denmark. We lead and contribute to national and multinational studies in close collaboration with clinicians, statisticians, patient organizations and research partners across the Nordic countries and Europe.

Our research

  • PROs in clinical trials

    Designing, collecting and analyzing patient-reported outcomes in randomized and single-arm trials, following international standards for PRO endpoints and estimands.

  • Instrument development and validation

    Developing and psychometrically validating questionnaires so that what matters to patients can be measured reliably, using COSMIN-aligned methodology.

  • Multiple myeloma

    Quality of life during modern myeloma treatment, including measurable residual disease (MRD) guided strategies and long-term maintenance.

  • Autoimmune cytopenias

    Measuring the symptom burden of immune thrombocytopenia (ITP), autoimmune hemolytic anemia (AIHA) and their treatment, including corticosteroids.

  • Older patients and frailty

    Using geriatric screening tools such as the G8 to understand how frailty relates to treatment tolerance and outcomes in older patients with cancer.

  • Patient involvement

    Working with patients and patient organizations so that our questions, methods and results reflect what matters most to the people we study.

Advice and collaboration

Planning a study with patient-reported outcomes? We advise on choice of instruments, PRO endpoints, statistical analysis plans and grant applications, and welcome new collaborations.

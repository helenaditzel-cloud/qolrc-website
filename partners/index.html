Partners

We work with clinical, academic and patient partners in Denmark and abroad.

  • Denmark

    Department of Hematology, Odense University Hospital

    Our home department.

    Visit website: Department of Hematology, Odense University Hospital

  • Denmark

    University of Southern Denmark

    Academic affiliation.

    Visit website: University of Southern Denmark

  • Nordic countries

    Nordic Myeloma Study Group (NMSG)

    Collaborative group for clinical myeloma research in Norway, Sweden, Finland, Estonia, Iceland and Denmark. QoLRC is the group's core facility for quality of life research in clinical protocols.

    Visit website: Nordic Myeloma Study Group (NMSG)

  • Nordic countries

    Nordic ITP Group

    Nordic network for research and education in immune thrombocytopenia, with members from Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway and Sweden. Henrik Frederiksen is a member of the steering committee.

    Visit website: Nordic ITP Group

  • Denmark

    OPEN, Open Patient data Explorative Network

    Data management and biostatistics, including our collaboration with Professor Sören Möller, Department of Health Services Research, University of Southern Denmark.

Our funders

Our research is made possible by grants from these foundations and organizations.

QoLRC is part of the Department of Hematology at Odense University Hospital and is affiliated with the University of Southern Denmark.

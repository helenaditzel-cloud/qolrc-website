PRO expertise

The patient-reported outcome measures we work with, and how we can help you use them in your study.

Questionnaires we work with

  • EORTC QLQ-C30

    Core questionnaire for health-related quality of life in cancer, covering function, symptoms and global health status.

    More information: EORTC QLQ-C30 →

  • EORTC QLQ-MY20

    Module for patients with multiple myeloma, covering disease symptoms, side effects, body image and future perspective.

    More information: EORTC QLQ-MY20 →

  • EORTC QLQ-CIPN20

    Module for chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy.

    More information: EORTC QLQ-CIPN20 →

  • PROMIS-29

    Generic profile measure covering physical function, anxiety, depression, fatigue, sleep, social participation and pain.

    More information: PROMIS-29 →

  • Steroid Symptom Questionnaire (SSQ)

    Questionnaire on symptoms related to corticosteroid treatment, currently being validated in ITP and AIHA.

  • G8

    Geriatric screening tool used to identify older patients with cancer who may benefit from a full geriatric assessment.

How we can help

  • Choosing instruments

    Selecting PRO measures that fit your research question, population and setting.

  • PRO endpoints and protocols

    Defining PRO objectives, endpoints, estimands and assessment schedules, following SPIRIT-PRO.

  • Statistical analysis plans

    Analysis of longitudinal PRO data, including mixed models for repeated measures and handling of missing data.

  • Psychometric validation

    Developing and validating questionnaires and translations, following COSMIN methodology.

  • Data collection in practice

    Setting up electronic and paper-based PRO collection and monitoring missing responses in real time.

  • Grant applications

    Writing the PRO sections of grant applications and study protocols.

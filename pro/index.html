PRO expertise

The patient-reported outcome measures we work with, and how we can help you use them in your study.

Questionnaires we work with

Based on published work by QoLRC members. The badge marks questionnaires our team has translated or validated in Danish.

Cancer-specific quality of life

  • EORTC QLQ-C30Core questionnaire for health-related quality of life in cancer, including the summary score. Our paper
  • EORTC QLQ-MY20Module for multiple myeloma. Our paper
  • EORTC QLQ-CIPN20Module for chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy. Our paper
  • EORTC QLU-C10DUtility measure derived from the QLQ-C30, used for quality-adjusted survival. Our paper

Hematology-specific

  • SSQ-MM Danish validationSteroid Symptom Questionnaire for multiple myeloma: side effects of corticosteroid treatment. Translated into Danish and Norwegian by our team. Our paper
  • SSQ (ITP and AIHA)Steroid Symptom Questionnaire, to be validated in immune thrombocytopenia and autoimmune hemolytic anemia in a study in its early phase.
  • HM-PRO Danish validationGeneric patient-reported outcome measure for hematologic malignancies. Our paper
  • Lee cGVHD Symptom Scale Danish validationSymptoms of chronic graft-versus-host disease after allogeneic stem cell transplantation. Our paper

Symptoms and side effects

  • FACIT-FatigueCancer-related fatigue. Our paper
  • FACT-PSIPulmonary symptoms such as shortness of breath. Our paper
  • FACT/GOG-NtxTreatment-related neurotoxicity. Our paper
  • PRO-CTCAEPatient-reported adverse events in cancer treatment. Our paper

Generic health and well-being

  • PROMIS-29Profile of physical function, anxiety, depression, fatigue, sleep, social participation and pain.
  • SF-12Short generic health survey. Our paper
  • HADSHospital Anxiety and Depression Scale. Our paper
  • GAD-7Anxiety, used in an adapted version during COVID-19. Our paper

Care experience and caregivers

  • CTM-15 and PACT-M Danish validationPatient-reported experience of care transitions. Our paper
  • Caregiver Roles and Responsibilities Scale Danish validationThe tasks and roles of family caregivers of people with cancer. Our paper

Older patients and frailty

  • G8 and modified G8 Danish validationGeriatric screening tools to identify frailty in older patients with cancer. Our paper
  • EORTC QLQ-ELD14Module for older patients with cancer. Our paper
  • Tilburg Frailty IndicatorSelf-reported frailty. Our paper

How we can help

  • Choosing instruments

    Selecting PRO measures that fit your research question, population and setting.

  • PRO endpoints and protocols

    Defining PRO objectives, endpoints, estimands and assessment schedules, following SPIRIT-PRO.

  • Statistical analysis plans

    Analysis of longitudinal PRO data, including mixed models for repeated measures and handling of missing data.

  • Psychometric validation

    Developing and validating questionnaires and translations, following COSMIN methodology.

  • Data collection in practice

    Setting up electronic and paper-based PRO collection and monitoring missing responses in real time.

  • Grant applications

    Writing the PRO sections of grant applications and study protocols.
